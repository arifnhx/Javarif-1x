# Marmalade — Pilot Cold Open · Google Flow Pack (Omni 1.1 Flash)

Format: 9:16 vertical · ~44s · 9 shots · multi-cam sitcom look · live studio audience

---

## 1. Characters (Flow character / ingredient setup)

Create each as a character in Flow, attach the image, and paste the voice line into its voice field.
Reuse the **exact same descriptor text** in every shot prompt. That's what keeps them consistent.

### MARMALADE (the cat, lead)
- **Look:** chunky solid smoky-grey British Shorthair, round amber eyes, tiny black satin bow tie. Real cat, real proportions.
- **Voice:** deep, slow, velvety American baritone; smug lounge-singer cool; never raises his voice; dry, unbothered timing.
- **Image prompt (character sheet):**
  > Character turnaround sheet, photorealistic real cat: a chunky solid smoky-grey British Shorthair with round amber eyes wearing a tiny black satin bow tie. Plain warm light-grey backdrop. Five views in a row: front, three-quarter, profile, back, and an extreme close-up of his face with a smug half-lidded look. Same fur and bow tie in every view. No text.

### DUCHESS (the date)
- **Look:** elegant fluffy pure-white Persian, blue eyes, thin pearl necklace.
- **Voice:** posh, refined female voice with a light British accent; cool, slow, suspicious; every word sounds like a raised eyebrow.
- **Image prompt:**
  > Character turnaround sheet, photorealistic real cat: an elegant fluffy pure-white Persian with blue eyes and a thin pearl necklace. Plain warm light-grey backdrop. Five views in a row: front, three-quarter, profile, back, and an extreme close-up with a haughty side-eye. No text.

### DANI (the human)
- **Look:** woman about 30, messy brown top bun, oversized heather-grey hoodie, black leggings, grey socks, brown slippers, holding a closed cardboard pizza box.
- **Voice:** American woman about 30; warm but frazzled; fast, expressive, comedic; her voice cracks up high when outraged.
- **Image prompt:**
  > Character turnaround sheet, photorealistic: a woman about 30 with a messy brown top bun, oversized heather-grey hoodie, black leggings, grey socks and brown slippers, holding a closed pizza box. Plain warm light-grey backdrop. Front full body, three-quarter, profile, back, and a close-up with a shocked, exasperated face. No text.

### SET (location ingredient)
  > A cozy city apartment sitcom set, photorealistic, warm multi-camera sitcom lighting. A small round dining table with a white tablecloth, two lit candles, a red rose, two small plates of salmon and two glasses of milk, two wooden chairs. The front door opens from a hallway; a small side table just inside the door; bookshelf, lamps, couch with a knitted throw, a record player on a cabinet. No people, no animals, no text.

## 2. Shots (Omni 1.1 Flash, 9:16, 1080p)

Attach to every shot: **Marmalade + Duchess + Dani + Set** (skip any character who isn't in frame).
Audio rule for every shot: *live studio audience in a sitcom taping; soft jazz from a record player in the room.*

### S1 · Door reveal (6s)
> One continuous shot from the hallway, behind Dani's shoulder. She pushes the apartment door open with her hip, carrying a pizza box, and the camera glides forward past her shoulder to reveal Marmalade and Duchess having a candlelit dinner at the little table, both sitting upright on chairs. She stops dead. Dani says, "Wait, what—" Warm sitcom lighting, soft jazz playing in the room.

### S2 · Dani close-up (4s)
> Medium close-up of Dani in the doorway, clutching the pizza box, blinking in disbelief. She says, confused and wounded, "...you said we were doing movie night!" The studio audience goes "Ooooooh!" Static camera.

### S3 · The ten bucks (8s)
> Slow push-in on Marmalade at the candlelit table. He turns his head over his shoulder toward Dani with a smug half-lidded side-eye and slides a folded ten-dollar bill across the tablecloth with one paw. In his deep, velvety voice, lowered to a whisper: "Psst. Here's ten bucks. Go see a movie." Beat. "Alone." The audience laughs. "Don't disturb my date. Go." He turns back to Duchess as if nothing happened.

### S4 · Low angle protest (4s)
> Low-angle shot looking up at Dani in the doorway, pizza box in one arm, the other hand thrown up. She says, outraged, voice cracking, "But... this is MY house!" Static camera.

### S5 · Duchess side-eye (4s)
> Close-up on Duchess across the candlelit table, rack focus from the rose in the foreground to her face. She slowly turns her head to Marmalade and says, posh and suspicious, "You said you owned the place."

### S6 · The kill line (4s)
> Extreme close-up on Marmalade's face, candlelight in his amber eyes. He doesn't blink, doesn't look at Dani. Deep, flat, unbothered: "She's the help." Locked-off camera.

### S7 · Reaction punch-in (3s)
> Fast punch-in on Dani's face as her jaw drops in silent outrage. The studio audience explodes with laughter and a long "OHHHHH!"

### S8 · Leave the pizza (7s)
> Wide locked-off shot of the room, one continuous take. Dani sighs, shoulders slumping, and turns to leave with the pizza box. Without looking up from his date, Marmalade says, deep and bossy, "Leave the pizza." Dani freezes, sets the box on the little side table by the door, steps out and shuts the door. The audience laughs, then applauds and whoops.

### S9 · Tag / loop (4s)
> Close-up of Marmalade. He slowly turns and looks straight into the camera, gives one long, satisfied slow blink, then nudges the pizza box toward Duchess with his paw. Soft jazz swells, the audience gives a final "Awww" and applause.
